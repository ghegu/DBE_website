# DBE — Design for the Built Environment (MSc LM-12)

Official interactive web concept for the international Master of Science in **Design for the Built Environment (DBE)** at **DiARC, Università degli Studi di Napoli Federico II** (A.Y. 2026/2027), translating the physical printed editorial identity into a clear, playful digital experience.

## Highlights
- **Visual Identity:** Faithful Swiss editorial palette (`#ED1C24`, `#2E3192`, `#00AEEF`, `#FFF200`), Helvetica Neue Black & Light typography, and original vector assets extracted from the editorial booklet.
- **Interactive Editorial Artifacts:**
  - Bruno Munari speaking & folded forks (Pages 2–3)
  - Red / Blue Optical Acetate Duality Lens for **Exhibit Design (DB102)** vs **Digital Design (DB103)** (Pages 4–5)
  - Cobalt Die-Cut Window + Expandable Yellow 6-Skills Foldout & Editorial Bookmark (Pages 7–9)
  - Translucent Tracing Paper overlay (`READY TO SHAPE YOUR FUTURE?`) over Federico II stats & requirements (Page 11)
  - Interactive Sticker Atelier (`TAKE ME WITH YOU!`) with high-resolution PNG & PDF postcard export (Page 6)
  - Interactive Mirror Camera Photobooth (`THE FUTURE NEEDS DESIGNERS. ARE YOU IN?`) with PNG & PDF portrait export (Page 12)
  - Living **DBE Mascotte Virtual Assistant** with section-aware moods, bilingual EN/IT Q&A, and spatial page navigation
- **Verified 2026/2027 Academic Info:** Full 120 ECTS Study Plan, 30-second Admissions Eligibility Checker (Italian / EU / Extra-EU), Palazzo Gravina campus details, faculty, and bilingual `EN / IT` support.
