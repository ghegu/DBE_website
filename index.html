PAGE 03 · IDENTITY

A SPACE IS NEVER
JUST A SPACE.

The world around us is constantly evolving. Spaces, objects, technologies and human behaviours shape the environments we experience every day.

Design for the Built Environment explores these connections through a multidisciplinary approach at DiARC, University of Naples Federico II, combining design culture with technological, social and creative knowledge.

Multidisciplinary DNA Architecture, industrial design, engineering, visual arts and digital media under one roof.
Studio Driven Over 60% hands-on integrated studios, workshops, prototyping and 15 ECTS synthesis ateliers.
Global in Naples 100% English international cohort inside a 16th century palace in historic Naples.

Exhibit Design · DB102

YEAR 2 · 60 ECTS
  • Focus: Museums, temporary pavilions, pop-up architecture, retail, interiors and cultural heritage.
  • Core Studios: Digital Manufacturing Studio (10), Exhibit Design for Interiors & Lighting (10), Atelier of Temporary Spaces (15).
  • Tools: Parametric and generative modeling, CNC and 3D digital fabrication, museography, photometric lighting design.
Inspect Exhibit Courses →

Digital Design · DB103

YEAR 2 · 60 ECTS
  • Focus: Interaction Design (UI/UX), digital products and services, HCI, AR/VR, branding and connected IoT spaces.
  • Core Studios: Digital Design & Aesthetics Studio (10), Systemic Design & Web Branding (10), Atelier of Interaction Design (15).
  • Tools: User centered prototyping, data processing systems, multimedia storytelling, systemic service design.
